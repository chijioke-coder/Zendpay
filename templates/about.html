Zendpay is a real-world simulation of a digital wallet. All balances and transfers are virtual. No real money is stored or moved, and balances have no monetary value.

Transfers to Zendpay accounts and owner-supplied bank accounts are supported. Anything else shows "Service unavailable for this financial institution".
